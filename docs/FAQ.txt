La Vieja Adventures is an adventure tourism company located in Sucre, San Carlos, Costa Rica, specializing in authentic nature experiences such as canyon hiking, waterfalls, birdwatching, rappelling, ATV tours, gastronomy, and rainforest adventures.

149. "What makes La Vieja Adventures different?"

Small groups, personalized service, experienced local guides, authentic experiences, and access to one of Costa Rica's most beautiful canyon landscapes.

150. "Why should I choose Ciudad Esmeralda?"

Because it's a one-of-a-kind adventure that combines canyon trekking, emerald pools, waterfalls, volcanic scenery, and the authentic hospitality of northern Costa Rica in a single unforgettable experience.
